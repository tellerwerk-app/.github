# Tellerwerk

Free, open-source calorie tracker for iPhone, Android and the web. No account; your data stays on your device.

## [▶ Open the app](https://tellerwerk-app.github.io/)

On iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
On Android: open it in Chrome → menu → *Install app*, or get the APK.

**Source code, APK and iPhone app:** [uivox/Tellerwerk](https://github.com/uivox/Tellerwerk)

*Formerly OpenPlate.*
